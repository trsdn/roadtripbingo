# Road Trip Bingo — Game Rules

*Print this page and keep it in the glove compartment.*

## Objective

Be the first player to spot enough objects along the road to complete a line on
your bingo card — and call **"Bingo!"**

## Preparation

1. Open the generator and choose a grid size (3x3 up to 8x8; 5x5 is the classic).
2. Pick the icon set, number of card sets, and cards per set (one card per player).
3. Click **Generate Bingo Cards**, review the preview, then **Download PDF** and print.
4. Hand out one card and a pen per player. The driver navigates — they don't play.

## Standard Rules

- When you spot an object shown on your card in the real world (a cow, a tractor,
  a wind turbine…), mark that square with your pen.
- Each real-world sighting counts for **one** square only. If two players see the
  same object, both may mark it.
- The first player to complete a full **row, column, or diagonal** calls
  **"Bingo!"** and wins the round.
- Disputed sightings are settled by the co-driver (or by majority vote).

### Free Square (Center Blank)

If the cards were generated with the **center blank** option (available for odd grids: 3x3, 5x5,
and 7x7 grids), the middle square is a free square: it counts as already
marked for every player.

## Variants

### Blackout / Full House

Instead of a single line, players must mark **every** square on the card.
Ideal for long stretches of highway.

### Multi-Hit Mode

Cards generated with **Multi-Hit Mode** have some squares with a small counter
(e.g. "3x"). These objects must be spotted **that many times** before the square
counts as marked — tick off one hit per sighting. The generator offers three
multi-hit intensities:

| Level  | Multi-hit squares | Hits required per square |
|--------|-------------------|--------------------------|
| Light  | 20–30% of squares | 2–3 sightings            |
| Medium | 40–50% of squares | 2–4 sightings            |
| Hard   | 60–70% of squares | 3–5 sightings            |

Easier-to-spot icons are preferred for multi-hit squares, so the game stays fair.

## Fairness Tips for Families

- **Identical cards** (enable *"Use identical card for all players"*): everyone
  hunts for the same objects — pure speed and attention decide. Best for mixed
  ages, since no one gets an unlucky card.
- **Different cards** (default): each player gets a unique card — more suspense,
  and players can't simply copy a neighbor's markings.
- Younger children can play with a smaller grid (3x3 or 4x4) while adults use
  5x5 — generate separate sets per skill level.
- Agree before departure what counts (e.g. does a cow on a billboard count?).

## Difficulty Levels (Game Difficulty Setting)

The **Game Difficulty** setting controls how hard-to-spot the icons on the card
are (every icon in the library has a difficulty rating):

| Setting | Icon mix on the card                          |
|---------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Easy    | ~50% easy, 35% medium, 15% hard icons         |
| Medium  | ~25% easy, 50% medium, 25% hard icons         |
| Hard    | ~15% easy, 35% medium, 50% hard icons         |
| Expert  | ~10% easy, 20% medium, 70% hard icons         |

Combine a higher game difficulty with Multi-Hit Mode (Hard) and Blackout rules
for the ultimate long-distance challenge.
